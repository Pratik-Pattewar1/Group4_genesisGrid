## Leakage guard rules

- **Forbidden always:** `delivery_person_id`, `order_date`, `order_datetime`, `order_id`, `order_time`, `pickup_datetime`, `pickup_time`, `time_taken_min`; any column matching `_raw$`, `^dq_`, `pickup`, `time_taken`, `(^|_)late($|_)`, `target`.
- **Excluded from the primary model (owner decision):** `delivery_person_age`, `delivery_person_rating`, `vehicle_condition`, `vehicle_type`, `multiple_deliveries`, `weather`, `traffic_density`, `city_code`.
- Missing values are kept in the dataset. Imputation happens only inside `build_preprocessor()`, fitted on the training split.
- No missing-indicator features (e.g. Festival-missing, order-time-missing) are created.
